## Brand & Style

The brand personality balances early childhood wonder with clinical and pedagogical rigor. It conveys warmth, joy, safety, and modern developmental excellence for Peruvian families seeking elite early education, pediatric therapies, and sensory play.

The design movement combines **Modern Tactile Softness** with **Playful Geometric Minimalism**:
- **Tactile Softness:** Pill-shaped interactive elements, squishy micro-affordances, and generous radii evoke tactile wooden blocks, developmental toys, and protective padded corners.
- **Airy Optimism:** Ample clean white space counterbalances an energetic multi-color palette, avoiding visual chaos while maintaining high legibility and institutional trust.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Subtle buoyant bobs, squishy press states, and gentle ambient glows create a delightful, approachable digital touchpoint for busy parents and caretakers.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional connection of joy, optimism, and safety:
- **Primary Turquoise (`#38C5FD`):** Represents clarity, vitality, sensory openness, and discovery. Used for key navigational nodes, primary focal highlights, active states, and reassurance cues.
- **Secondary Vibrant Pink (`#E25192`):** Communicates affection, warmth, and active play. Dedicated to high-conversion actions, enrollment CTAs, special milestones, and key lead-capture alerts.
- **Tertiary Lime Green (`#B2D169`):** Evokes organic growth, nature, vitality, and motor development. Utilized for milestones, physical activity badges, validation states, and sensory workshop tags.
- **Accent Sunshine Yellow (`#EEB041`):** Represents intellectual stimulation, creativity, and warmth. Applied selectively to badges, star reviews, seasonal announcements, and AI assistant accents.
- **Neutral Dark Slate (`#1E293B`):** Supplies grounding structure and exceptional legibility across body text and deep navigation headers, steering clear of stark pure black.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`):** Pure white serves as the dominant structural base, paired with ultra-light slate tint `#F8FAFC` for alternate content tiers.

## Typography

The type system blends the organic roundness of **Quicksand** with the highly functional readability of **Nunito Sans**:
- **Quicksand (Headings & Display):** Naturally softened terminals evoke child-centric approachability while bold weights maintain clear authority. Applied to hero statements, module titles, and celebratory milestones.
- **Nunito Sans (Body, Forms & UI Labels):** Delivers open counters, legible letterforms, and warm humanist traits. Provides fatigue-free reading for pedagogical descriptions, evaluation metrics, and admission requirements.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Display headlines employ snug line-heights and slight negative tracking to preserve structural rhythm. Labels use subtle positive tracking and elevated weights to guarantee instant scanning on smaller mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured, responsive grid ensures seamless readability across mobile devices and desktop screens:
- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column fluid grid with a maximum container width of `1280px`, dynamic outer margins of `2.5rem`, and gutters of `1.5rem`. Tablet layouts switch to an 8-column layout, while mobile relies on a 4-column structure with `1.25rem` outer margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Content modules sit within deliberate breathing zones spaced at `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to `space-xxl` (`4rem`), allowing sections to feel organized, stress-free, and uncluttered.
- **Padded Safeguards:** Card padding consistently uses `space-lg` to `space-xl`, ensuring content never crowds curved structural borders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids rigid, dark drop shadows in favor of tinted, feather-light ambient diffusion:
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and muted tint `#F8FAFC` for foundational canvas surfaces.
- **Level 1 (Interactive Base / Cards):** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05)`, accompanied by a subtle `#E2E8F0` hairline border (50% opacity) for crisp boundary definition.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Floating Cards / Dropdowns):** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(56, 197, 253, 0.12)`, utilizing a primary turquoise tint to produce a bright, luminous lift.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Sticky Lead Badges):** `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(226, 81, 146, 0.15)`, carrying an energetic pink undertone for urgent lead capture and overlay sheets.
- **Tactile Pressed State:** Drop shadows collapse to `0px 1px 3px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)` accompanied by a `scale(0.98)` transform for squishy, responsive feedback.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Pill and Rounded-3XL** aesthetic:
- **Buttons, Badges & Micro-Pills:** Fully circular and pill geometries (`border-radius: 9999px`) provide safe, approachable touchpoints that invite interaction.
- **Containers, Program Cards & Modals:** Standardized around extra-large radii (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`), echoing rounded furniture and sensory play zones.
- **Asymmetric Playful Accents:** Distinctive brand components feature organic alternating corner radii (e.g., `rounded-tl-3xl rounded-br-3xl rounded-tr-lg rounded-bl-lg`) to punctuate educational pathways and gallery previews.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Enrollment & Conversion):** Vibrant Pink `#E25192` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, Quicksand font weight 700, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). Generous padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Subtle hover scale (`scale(1.02)`) with a warm pink glow shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Workshops & Tours):** Primary Turquoise `#38C5FD` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, pill-shaped, turquoise ambient shadow on hover.
- **Soft Accent / Tonal Button:** Light-tinted backgrounds (e.g., `#38C5FD` at 12% opacity) paired with `#0284C7` text for lower priority actions.
- **Tactile Feedback:** Active states trigger a slight downward push (`translate-y-0.5` and `scale(0.98)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Milestone & Stage Chips:** Pill-shaped tags with matching 15% opacity tint backgrounds and solid text:
  - Toddlers/Sensory: Lime Green background `#B2D169` (15%) + text `#4D6B10`.
  - Preschool/Cognitive: Turquoise background `#38C5FD` (15%) + text `#0369A1`.
  - Creative/Art: Sunshine Yellow background `#EEB041` (15%) + text `#92400E`.
- **Conversion Pill Badge:** Floating badge for urgency ("Cupos Limitados 2025" / "Vacantes Disponibles") set in Vibrant Pink with white text and a gentle pulsing dot indicator.

### Lead Capture & Admission Cards
- Elevated card container with `rounded-3xl` (`1.75rem`), pure white background `#FFFFFF`, and Level 2 soft shadow.
- Top decorative visual accent featuring a smooth 4px gradient border spanning Turquoise to Pink.
- Built-in quick contact buttons (e.g., direct WhatsApp trigger in brand lime or primary pink).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#F8FAFC`, border: 1.5px solid `#E2E8F0`, corner radius: `1rem` (`rounded-2xl`).
- Padding: `0.875rem 1.25rem`. Text: `#1E293B`, placeholder: `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Smooth transition to `#FFFFFF` background, solid border in `#38C5FD`, and a 3px ring of `#38C5FD` at 20% opacity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radios and checkboxes feature generous sizing (22px) with `rounded-lg` or full circle styling.
- Checked state: Filled with `#38C5FD` or `#E25192` featuring a crisp white inner icon.

### Friendly AI Assistant ("RecreaBot" / Virtual Guide)
- **Floating Launcher:** Circular button (`64px x 64px`) floating bottom-right, colored in Turquoise `#38C5FD` with a soft Sunshine Yellow `#EEB041` badge indicator. Features a joyful waving character or chat spark icon.
- **Chat Window:** Window panel with `rounded-3xl` curvature, pure white interior, and an ultra-soft glassmorphic header (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `#38C5FD` with white text).
- **Dialogue Bubbles:** Assistant messages utilize `#F1F5F9` with dark slate text and an asymmetrical pill corner (`rounded-2xl rounded-tl-sm`). Parent messages utilize `#38C5FD` with crisp white text (`rounded-2xl rounded-tr-sm`).
- **Interactive Prompts:** Rounded pill suggestion pills ("Conocer metodología", "Costos de matrícula", "Agendar visita guiada") styled with `#F8FAFC` background, hairline border, and interactive pink hover state.
